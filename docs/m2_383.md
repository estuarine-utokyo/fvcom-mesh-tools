## Design and evidence

A uses `TokyoBay_dep_m7001tp_rfac0p2_cap300.dat`, confirmed by MESH.md,
mesh_config.yaml and the current b12 MANIFEST.md: M7001, T.P. correction,
rx0 smoothing and a 300 m cap. The archived b12 namelist is absent here;
the available inherited `hydro/tuning/new_bc/tb_multiyear_p1a9_run.nml`
sets EXTSTEP_SECONDS=5, ISPLIT=10, roughness=0.002693138 m and minimum Cd=0.003.
B originates from SRTM15 Kanto (notebooks/325_sample_repro.py), minimum 2 m.
B_own retains those positive-down depths; the exporter does not negate them.
B_Adepth uses linear interpolation on A's nodes, nearest outside their convex hull;
36 nodes needed nearest fallback. No additional depth clipping/smoothing is applied.

Measured in the repository-local staging check:

|---|---|---|---|

| B_Adepth | 3393 / 5849 | 3–300 | 15.4221 |

These use QA's minimum-edge / sqrt(g * maximum element depth) formula.
Full QA gates were not rerun. Export format validation passed for B.
All have 13 OBC nodes; B's maximum offset from A's polyline is 5.295 m.
The common external step is 5 s (production), ISPLIT=10 (internal 50 s), with a 3x
linear CFL margin; actual nonlinear stability remains to be demonstrated.
Five uniform sigma layers; constant T=20 C/S=30 with inactive scalar evolution;
3-D closure mixing from the working hydro test, constant roughness/minimum Cd
as above, the production sponge (TokyoBay_spg.dat radii/coefficients mapped to each
mesh by along-arc position; added after job 115180 blew up near the OBC in all
three runs without it), no wind, heat, rain, rivers, groundwater, dye or nesting.
Eight MPI ranks per run; 11 days from 2021-01-01 UTC, tanh ramp timescale 1 day,
first 3 days discarded, 8 analysis days, output every 30 minutes. Four-hour allocation.

Forcing uses ONE published product: `tides/ptide/Tide-ToKYOWAN.txt`, read with
its existing ptide.py after inspecting ptide/README.md and TIDE_PROJECT_HANDOVER.md.
Aburatsubo/Mera M2 constants are projected onto A's arc; amplitude and unwrapped
phase interpolate by along-arc distance for all cases. No endpoint extension was needed.
The gauges lie 5.11/10.01 km off the arc: this is a coastal-gauge approximation,
not an offshore tidal atlas. It avoids weather/NTR contamination of existing OBC time series.
Public constants are available directly, so an OBC harmonic fit was unnecessary.
Nodal factors/astronomical arguments are frozen at 2021-01-01 UTC (09:00 JST),
using the product's convention. FVCOM and analysis both use A*cos(omega*t-phase).
ASCII spectral syntax, ordinal indexing and metre units were checked in mod_input.F,
mod_force.F and mod_obcs.F; its calendar time origin does not make it Julian time-series forcing.

Tokyo-Shibaura, Harumi, Chiba Light, Shinko and Yokosuka use published gauge coordinates.
Each run selects its nearest node remaining wet throughout; distances are reported.
Fits include mean, M2, M4 and M6. Observational references are published harmonics
with the same frozen corrections, not contemporaneous observations. Pairwise differences
and half-window changes are reported. No quantitative match tolerance was supplied.
The figure uses use_readable_style/add_atlas_grid, common scales and all three meshes.
Health includes all numeric-field NaN/mask checks, output completeness, max |zeta|,
max |u|, horizontal speed, volume change and detided volume trend. Mass uses rho=1025.
Open-boundary storage changes are not a flux-budget conservation residual;
30-minute extrema also require inspection of FVCOM logs for intermediate failures.
